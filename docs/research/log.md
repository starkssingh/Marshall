# Research log

One entry per closed experiment, appended by `xq exp close` (EXP-005, ADR 0039) when the
experiment's conclusion is recorded: its verdict (supported, rejected or inconclusive) and the
Observed / Evidence / Interpretation / Limitations / Action fields. Entries are appended, never
edited or removed; a later experiment that revises a conclusion gets its own entry. `xq exp audit`
lists experiments that are still open, which must be none at the end of a sprint.
